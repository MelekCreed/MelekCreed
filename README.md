## About Me

I build intelligent software systems that connect **machine learning**, **backend engineering**, and **product-facing interfaces** into one coherent experience.

My work sits at the intersection of:

- AI engineering and applied machine learning
- full-stack product development with FastAPI, React, and Flutter
- retrieval-augmented generation and NLP-driven workflows
- decision systems, analytics dashboards, and production-oriented AI tools
- HR Tech and operational software where AI has to be useful, not decorative

I care about systems that are both technically credible and actually usable: clean APIs, strong product thinking, interpretable ML, and interfaces that make complex outputs feel actionable.
